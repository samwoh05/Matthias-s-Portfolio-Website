Sheet 02 / 06 — About

Show your working.

"why product design?" — answered the long way

Q2. Why product design?  [show all working]

Given ①

I need to make things. I pick up hobbies just to have somewhere new to explore an idea and see where it goes.

Given ②

I like carrying part of the load. Leading teams, volunteering — whatever makes things lighter for someone else.

∴
Product design. Inventing new things for someone else's benefit — to make a life a little easier. Nothing excites me more.
1

Perfectionist, a little.

I question every small detail of a design and find a justification for each corner — until it's foolproof. Try it: measure the 12.00 mm pin.

15.20 mm Slide the jaw onto the pin. Arrow keys move 0.01 mm.
2

Team first.

Communication is never the problem. I make sure the whole team stays on the same page — nobody drifting off to do their own thing.

3

110%, promised.

Creative work isn't a chore to get finished. I put everything in, because the point is to genuinely innovate for someone else.

“Details make perfection, and perfection is not a detail.”— Leonardo da Vinci
